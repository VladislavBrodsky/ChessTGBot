01 / The campaign architecture

Every creative uses six layers: canvas, brand, hook, evidence, action, and supporting terms. Keep one idea and one action on each card. The artwork creates the first visual impression; the evidence explains why someone should act. Choose the family by the campaign’s objective.

04 / From Story to short video

The attached references work through oversized chess objects and staggered cards. Recreate that rhythm with real product footage and short, finite entrances. A suggested 8-second sequence:

0.0–1.5sHook is fully readable on frame one. Sculpture moves 12–24px into its position.One reason to pause.

5.5–8.0sHold a stable final action frame. Leave space for the native link or CTA.One next move.

For puzzles, give viewers longer to solve before the reveal. Capture the current Mini App with its Obsidian styling and place that footage inside the marketing composition. Never show a decorative marketing card as though it were shipped app UI. Use captions for essential speech; source licensed audio when adding sound. A static version must communicate the same idea without animation.

05 / Test the reason to act

Use the alternate hook in the studio for an initial A/B comparison. Hold the audience, format, proof, destination and campaign objective steady. Compare the same measurement window. Start with the result that matches the family: saves for lessons, real move comments for puzzles, invite creation for friend challenges, and game starts for acquisition.

Record creative name, format, hook variant, publication date, reach, shares, saves, link visits and the relevant product action. Treat missing analytics as unavailable. Where attribution is not implemented, use platform engagement and aggregate product trends until a real tracking path exists. The kit includes measurement targets, not new analytics instrumentation.

A hook can earn attention.
Value earns the return.

These designs are built to encourage shares, saves and participation. Virality is an outcome to measure, not a promise to put on the creative.

06 / Brand and product accuracy

Use the existing Barlow Condensed / Onest typography, current open violet rook / outlined CHESS lockup, contextual sculptures and Phosphor regular icons. Use warm gray, paper and black as surfaces, with small mint accents. Do not fill a canvas with Voltage. The studio provides Ink as a marketing statement surface. It does not replace the Mini App’s Obsidian theme.

Practice and puzzle content may state that those activities are free. Friend and PvP promotions must keep their USDT stake terms. Settlement credits the platform balance; withdrawals are a separate flow. Re-check product copy when options change. Use real app screens and verified evidence instead of invented user counts, testimonials, rewards or payout claims.

Destinations in the kit use verified website URLs and supported Telegram links. To add tracked Mini App campaigns, reserve a real marketing card ID through the existing backend workflow. Never invent a startapp value: unrecognized values can be treated as a game ID.

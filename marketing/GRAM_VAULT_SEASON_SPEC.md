# 100 GRAM Vault — season design and launch contract

**Status:** product specification and Mini App preview only. The live `first-three-v1` box has no GRAM reward. No GRAM season, entry, prize reserve, token payout, or winner exists yet.

## Player promise and the chosen draw

One completed season issues exactly 1,000 free Mystery Boxes to 1,000 eligible accounts. Exactly one box position, selected uniformly from **801–1,000** before the season opens, contains **100 GRAM** in addition to its normal in-app reward. Boxes **1–800 have zero jackpot chance**. For any specific position in 801–1,000, the chance before the eligible phase begins is 1/200. If the prize has not appeared, the next position's conditional chance is `1 / (1,001 - position)`; for example, position 900 is 1/101 if positions 801–899 missed. The overall season has one winner only if it reaches the selected position. The 1/1,000 figure describes the share of all boxes that wins in a completed season; it is **not** every player's odds.

Public wording after launch: “One 100 GRAM jackpot is assigned to one box from positions 801–1,000 in each completed 1,000-box season. Boxes 1–800 cannot win the jackpot. Every box also gives an in-app reward. See current position and rules before opening.” Never say “every player has a chance”, “every box can win”, or imply that today's `first-three-v1` box contains GRAM. Until launch, say “Planned season” and show no join/open control, live counter, countdown, winner, or payout claim.

**Product tradeoff:** the first 800 players supply most of the season's progress but cannot win GRAM. If equal player opportunity is the goal, select the winner uniformly from all 1,000 boxes and reveal the result only after box 800; that gives each box a 1/1,000 chance. This is a different mechanic and requires new published rules. The current preview reflects the requested later-window mechanic.

## Player journey

1. **Discovery:** Home shows a separate, explicitly planned Vault card. The existing free box remains the only active offer. A details sheet explains the two phases and the fact that the current box cannot award GRAM.
2. **Future enrollment:** after launch, show the exact season ID, prize asset, eligibility, closing policy, current server-confirmed position, free qualification (three AI games of at least 20 plies after joining), one-entry-per-account rule, and linked full rules before the join action. No deposit, wager, purchase, or referral is required; paid activity does not increase odds.
3. **Progress:** use the server's game record to show `0/3 → 3/3`, then a claimable state. Display “Regular-reward phase; no GRAM jackpot chance” while positions 1–800 remain. At 801+, show the server-confirmed position and whether the jackpot remains. Never animate a guess while requests load.
4. **Opening:** claim with an idempotency key. The server returns the assigned box position, normal reward, jackpot result, season proof reference, and claim state. A normal reveal is calm; a **server-confirmed** GRAM win may use brief confetti and one success haptic. Reduced-motion users receive a static success state. A timeout shows “Checking your result” and retrieves the same receipt; it never redraws.
5. **Winner claim:** require an authenticated wallet address and explicit confirmation. Show pending, paid, or needs-attention states with a receipt/transaction link. The jackpot amount is a token quantity, not a fiat-value guarantee. Never mark a transfer paid from a broadcast timeout.
6. **Season close:** show the committed draw hash, revealed seed or independent audit attestation, winning position, total boxes issued, and payout status. Do not expose the winner's name or wallet without consent.

## Backend contract for the later implementation

- Create a **new campaign version** such as `gram-vault-s1`; do not mutate historic `first-three-v1` receipts or retroactively imply that earlier boxes entered. Use the same Mystery Box visual language but independent season entitlement and ledger. Existing players can join the new season under its published terms.
- `vault_seasons`: ID, rules version/hash, asset contract, amount, reserve transaction, state (`draft`, `funded`, `open`, `paused`, `closed`), `issued_count`, draw commitment, secure seed reference, selected position, start/end policy, timestamps. Require a verified 100 GRAM reserve before `open`.
- `vault_entries`: one per account/season, enrollment timestamp, qualification status. Count only completed, free, server-recorded AI games after enrollment; reject abandoned or paid games and duplicate game IDs.
- `vault_boxes`: unique `(season_id, position)`, unique `(season_id, user_id)`, unique source entitlement/idempotency key, ordinary reward receipt, jackpot result, opened timestamp. Under a database lock on the season row, assign the next position and commit the box receipt and normal reward together. Retries return that receipt.
- At season creation, use a cryptographically secure random seed and unbiased sampling to choose one integer in `[801,1000]`. Publish `SHA-256(season_id || rules_hash || seed)` before the first box; store the seed encrypted and restrict access. Reveal it after close so the position can be recomputed. Server operators must not be able to re-seed an open season.
- `vault_prize_claims`: one per winning season, GRAM amount and token master address, user ID, verified destination wallet, claim status, payout idempotency key, signed transfer reference and confirmed on-chain transaction. Reserve/claim and payout are distinct states. Reconcile uncertain transfers on-chain before retrying.
- Read API returns a **server-authoritative** public catalog (`enabled`, `season_id`, `rules_url`, `asset`, `amount`, `eligible_from`, `total_boxes`, `issued_count`, `jackpot_remaining`, `closing_policy`, `commitment_hash`) and authenticated entry/receipt state. The client must never generate counts, odds, winner status, or prize balances locally.
- Admin controls can pause new entries and openings while preserving existing entitlements. Configuration changes require a new rules version and season; prize quantity, target window, or eligibility cannot change silently mid-season.

## Notifications, trust, and measurement

Send at most one opt-in message for each meaningful state: challenge joined, box earned, eligible phase begins, confirmed win, claim reminder, payout confirmation, or season close. Deduplicate by `(season_id, user_id, event)`, honor mute/locale, and avoid “almost won” and false-urgency language. Channel posts use a reviewed image, the same rules URL, and a live catalog check immediately before sending. HTML-escape all Telegram names.

Instrument distinct stages: campaign impression, rules opened, join, first/third qualifying game, box claim, position assigned, confirmed jackpot, wallet claim, on-chain payment, referral arrival, and seven-day retained play. Record source and campaign IDs without assuming a Telegram click became a player. Report both engagement and prize cost; compare retained players against an unexposed cohort after enough data exists.

Before activating a worldwide token promotion, publish the exact token contract, funding proof, prize inventory, age/country eligibility, entry and closing policy (including what happens if fewer than 1,000 boxes open), winner verification, claim deadline, taxes/fees, disputes, and payout method. A random crypto prize can trigger different promotion rules by market; [UK Gambling Commission free-draw guidance](https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-and-players/guide/page/free-draws-and-prize-competitions/) and the [US FTC advertising guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/advertising-faqs-guide-small-business) both make clear why the qualifying terms and odds must be disclosed prominently. This is a launch requirement, not a statement that the proposed promotion is currently approved.

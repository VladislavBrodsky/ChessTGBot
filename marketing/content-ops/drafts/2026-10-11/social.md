## Telegram

**One lost move. One new habit.**

After a blitz loss, replay one decision that changed the game. Before looking at an engine, ask: What had your opponent just threatened? What did your move leave loose? What forcing reply followed?

In our example, 1...Rxd2 grabbed a pawn and left the back rank. White answered 2.Ra8+ Rd8 3.Rxd8#. The useful note is not “I blundered.” It is “check the back rank before moving its only defender.”

## Instagram caption

One lost move. One new habit.

You do not need to explain every move of your last blitz game. Pick one decision. Rewind. Look for their check or capture after your move. Then write a cue you could use in a future game.

Our board example: Black took the pawn with 1...Rxd2? White found 2.Ra8+ Rd8 3.Rxd8#. The lesson: when your king has no escape square, check what happens if its back-rank defender moves.

Save this for your next post-game review. Open the Academy from our profile to practise.
